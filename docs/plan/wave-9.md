# Wave 9: Free play UI, notifications, verify

**Goal.** A player can play a Free round in the browser: pick, seal, wait, watch the reveal. Notifications go out through every channel with dedupe. `@flocked/verify` reproduces any settlement from public data.

**Base.** Tag `wave-8`. **Hold point:** OA-D2 (core round flow frozen) before W9-C: frozen Oct 12, 2026. **Migrations reserved:** W9-A `0021`, W9-D `0022` (shifted by W7-Z, Oct 12: W7-F1 took `0020`).

## Pinned interfaces

- **Notification senders:** `apps/api/src/notify/channels/{farcaster,webpush,email}.ts` implement `Channel.send(batch)`. The queue consumer applies the dedupe keys from the spec. Copy comes from `packages/shared/src/copy/notifications.ts` (W6-C).
- **Verify library:** `@flocked/verify` exports `verifyRound({ rpcUrl, chainId, roundId, mode, bundleBaseUrl, drand? }) → Report` and `verifyEntry(...)`, and the CLI `flocked-verify` (bin name; package published as `@flocked/verify`). The web Verify page (W11-C) imports only the browser-safe subset `@flocked/verify/browser`.

## W9-B: `@flocked/verify` + CLI

- **Role / size:** build, M.
- **Objective.** Build the verification library and CLI. It fetches the manifest and chunks, checks chunk hashes and totals, verifies the drand signature for `beaconRound`, re-decrypts Free ciphertexts (Stakes ciphertexts from `Entered` logs), recomputes every VOID reason, re-runs `@flocked/settle` and both Merkle roots, compares against the onchain proposal (Stakes) or the anchored commitment and manifest (Free), and reports anchors from later-revoked keys as disputed. Exit codes: 0 match, 1 mismatch, 2 unable to verify.
- **Read first.** `plan.md` §2, §4. `docs/sessions/W8-Z.md`. This section. `docs/bundle-format.md`. Spec: "Settlement and payout math" (only "Settlement output"), "Sealed picks (timelock encryption)" (only "Invalid entries"), "Smart contract" (only the "Stolen anchor key" bullet).
- **Owns.** `packages/verify/**`.
- **Required tests.** PIPE-6 (fixtures: bundles generated by the W5-A pipeline in tests). Tamper tests: change one byte in a chunk, the root, a VOID reason or a tally, and each is detected. The browser subset builds without Node APIs.

- **Fixes** (each with a test that fails without it, mutation-checked):
  - **Guardian void** (owner decision, option a): the scheduler tick sweeps daily rounds whose Stakes mode became `voided` (`round_modes.final_at`) since its last pass and calls `enqueueVoidOutcomes` (`notify/outcomes.ts`); the consumer's dedupe drops repeats, so a window that overlaps the last pass is fine. Test: "a guardian `RoundRefunded(4)` tells each Stakes entrant once". W8-F1's `test/scheduler/tick-budget.test.ts` stays green with the step. The spec stays as written (Round lifecycle, `pending → voided`; Notifications, `refunded`).
  - **R2, proposed Stakes refunds** (`notify/consumer.ts` `laterRefund`, `notify/outcomes.ts` `notifyOnModeFinal`): Stakes reasons 1 and 6 are treated as final when they happen, but `OutcomeProposed` can carry reason 1 (VOID entries leave too few valid ones). Then a provisional rule-1 refund after the Free outcome is sent at once as `refunded` (2 h before claims open, while a veto is still possible), and its real finalization is deduped away; when Stakes took the outcome key first, the final `refunded` is never sent. Fix (spec, Notifications, Oct 16): a proposed Stakes refund is the `outcome` at the proposal only when it is the first, and `refunded` when it becomes final; a refund with no proposal (`refundTooFew`, timeout) stays as built. Tell the two apart without editing `indexer/handlers/stakes.ts` (for example from the mode's status or `claims_open_at` when the message is enqueued or claimed).
  - **R3, unfenced leases** (`notify/dedupe.ts` 11, 36–46, 69–83; `consumer.ts` 99, 125–150): the lease is `t + 60 s` with `t` read once per batch, no channel is marked until every send in the batch is done, the fetches have no timeout, and `finishKey` / `releaseKey` / `markDelivered` don't check the lease holder. A batch stalled past 60 s lets a duplicate reclaim the key and resend every channel, and the stalled batch then finishes or releases the new holder's lease. Fix: fence by a lease token (or `leased_until` read back), refresh the clock per message, and time out channel fetches.
  - **R4, merged accounts** (`consumer.ts` 282 → `render.ts` 79–81): the share is looked up with `e.user_id = <the account joined>`, but merges never re-point `entries`, so an outcome enqueued before a merge (or retried after one) is dropped as `notify_unrenderable`. Fix: render from the payload's original account, deliver to the joined one. Also record in the handoff (spec silent, for W10-Z): a merge doesn't move `push_subscriptions` or `notification_prefs`, so a device subscribed only under the merged-away account is never used again.
  - **Room successor** (owner decision, Oct 16; spec, Identity and personhood › Account deletion): a deleted owner's room passes to the oldest remaining active member, else the oldest suspended one; a merged or deleted account never takes it; with neither, the room closes with its unlocked rounds (`rooms/handover.ts`; W8-F1 allowed any non-deleted member).
  - **`memberCount`** (owner decision; spec, Rooms): the room view's count is the cap's (non-deleted members) (`rooms/detail.ts`).
  - **The archive's `revealing`** (owner decision; spec, API): `GET /rounds` shows a closed round's pending mode as `revealing` past beacon time, as `GET /rounds/:id` does (W8-F1's `asOfBeacon` in `read/rounds.ts`).
- **Required tests.** One per item, each mutation-checked; the handoff lists the mutation table. `pnpm check`, `pnpm format:check`, the stack tests and `pnpm e2e` (with `notifications.spec.ts`) on a fresh stack stay green. W10-D re-runs a sample on the merged tree.
