# Wave 9: Free play UI, notifications, verify

**Goal.** A player can play a Free round in the browser: pick, seal, wait, watch the reveal. Notifications go out through every channel with dedupe. `@flocked/verify` reproduces any settlement from public data.

**Base.** Tag `wave-8`. **Hold point:** OA-D2 (core round flow frozen) before W9-C. **Migrations reserved:** W9-A `0020`, W9-D `0021`.

## Pinned interfaces

- **Notification senders:** `apps/api/src/notify/channels/{farcaster,webpush,email}.ts` implement `Channel.send(batch)`. The queue consumer applies the dedupe keys from the spec. Copy comes from `packages/shared/src/copy/notifications.ts` (W6-C).
- **Verify library:** `@flocked/verify` exports `verifyRound({ rpcUrl, chainId, roundId, mode, bundleBaseUrl, drand? }) → Report` and `verifyEntry(...)`, and the CLI `flocked-verify` (bin name; package published as `@flocked/verify`). The web Verify page (W11-C) imports only the browser-safe subset `@flocked/verify/browser`.

## W9-A: Notifications

- **Role / size:** build, M.
- **Objective.** Build the `notify` queue consumer for every event in the spec's table, with defaults, timing and channels:
  - Farcaster mini app notifications (token storage from mini app events, rate limits, batching);
  - web push (VAPID, `POST /push/subscribe`);
  - email (Cloudflare Email Service binding);
  - `notification_prefs`;

  Wire RoundDO's alarms for `question_live` (only to users with no entry in the round that just closed), `closing_soon` and `streak_at_risk`. Also the daily official cast at `opensAt` (Farcaster signer; mocked locally).

## W9-B: `@flocked/verify` + CLI

- **Role / size:** build, M.
- **Objective.** Build the verification library and CLI. It fetches the manifest and chunks, checks chunk hashes and totals, verifies the drand signature for `beaconRound`, re-decrypts Free ciphertexts (Stakes ciphertexts from `Entered` logs), recomputes every VOID reason, re-runs `@flocked/settle` and both Merkle roots, compares against the onchain proposal (Stakes) or the anchored commitment and manifest (Free), and reports anchors from later-revoked keys as disputed. Exit codes: 0 match, 1 mismatch, 2 unable to verify.
- **Read first.** `plan.md` §2, §4. `docs/sessions/W8-Z.md`. This section. `docs/bundle-format.md`. Spec: "Settlement and payout math" (only "Settlement output"), "Sealed picks (timelock encryption)" (only "Invalid entries"), "Smart contract" (only the "Stolen anchor key" bullet).
- **Owns.** `packages/verify/**`.
- **Required tests.** PIPE-6 (fixtures: bundles generated by the W5-A pipeline in tests). Tamper tests: change one byte in a chunk, the root, a VOID reason or a tally, and each is detected. The browser subset builds without Node APIs.

## W9-C: Web Today, Sealed, Reveal

- **Role / size:** build (UI), M.
- **Objective.** Build the Today screen (question, option cards, Free stake presets + slider, mode toggle with Stakes hidden for now, countdown, live counts over the WS client, crowd-history hint, "Seal my pick"). The Free entry flow:
  - check the target round and lock leaf through a public RPC (`checkTargetRound`, `FlockedAnchor` lock leaf);
  - encrypt in the browser and POST;
  - verify the receipt (signer onchain and every field, commitment = keccak of own ciphertext);
  - store the pick and receipt locally; mismatches are shown and kept as evidence.

  Then the Sealed screen (pick or "Your pick is sealed" if storage was lost), the unsealing countdown, "Counting the flock…", and the reveal animation (3 s bars, accent on win, result card, mascot motion, `prefers-reduced-motion` fade, played once per round), the mode toggle on reveal, and "The next question is already live".
- **Read first.** `plan.md` §2. `docs/sessions/W8-Z.md`. This section. `docs/design/screens.md` (core: Today, Sealed, Reveal rows), their exports, and only those Paper artboards. `Design_Language.md` (Motion). Spec: "Client app" (Today, Sealed, Reveal rows; "Entry flow (Free)"), "Sealed picks (timelock encryption)" (only "Client requirements" and the client check bullets), "Real-time and the reveal" (only "Reveal choreography").
- **Owns.** `apps/web/src/features/{today,sealed,reveal}/**`, `apps/web/src/ws/**`, `apps/web/src/crypto/**`, `e2e/ui/{today,sealed,reveal,tlock-bundle}.spec.ts`. It notes route registrations for D.
- **Required tests.** TL-1 (browser half: the production bundle encrypts in Chromium, and the Worker decrypts with a recorded signature). Screenshot comparisons for every core-flow state it implements. Reduced-motion test (NFR-9 part). Receipt-mismatch handling. axe checks.
