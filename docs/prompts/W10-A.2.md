## Pinned interfaces

- R2 keys `cards/{roundId}/{mode}/{userId}/{kind}/{variant}.png` and
  `cards/{roundId}/{mode}/round/{variant}.png`; `share_cards` rows with opaque share IDs
  (`ShareIdSchema`), stable across a handle change or a re-render.
- `GET /rounds/:id/me` `cards` and `/s/:shareId` are what the web's Share already links (W9-C,
  `apps/web/src/features/reveal/ShareBlock.tsx`, which falls back to `/r/:id`): keep their shapes.
- `/rounds/:id/card/*` stays behind `requireRoundAccess` (W6-D). Reveal gating: no card or split
  exists before its mode's `revealed_at`.

## Tasks

1. `src/cards/build.ts`: one `CardModel` per (round, mode, user, kind) from D1 — the mode's status
   and refund reason, the valid tallies, the user's entry and result, the Stakes amount or multiple
   (amounts only if the user opted in: `prefs`, "show amounts on cards", default off), the stray
   streak at 2 or more, the handle (none after deletion), and the entrant count for the refund rules
   that replace the bars. Read nothing per-option before `revealed_at`.
2. `src/cards/consumer.ts`: the `cards` handler — build the model, render each requested variant,
   `BUCKET.put` at the pinned keys, upsert the `share_cards` row (new opaque share ID only when
   there is none), and be idempotent. Decide and record the queue's batch size and concurrency for
   the 5-minute target (see the handoff's "Open issues": ~150–350 ms a card).
3. `src/cards/enqueue.ts` and the enqueue points: every settled player's `result` card and the
   round card at the reveal fan-out (Free in `apply/free.ts`, Stakes at the confirmed proposal in
   `indexer/handlers/stakes.ts`), the veto re-render at the same keys, and the refund outcomes that
   never had a proposal. The deletion re-render already arrives from `identity/delete.ts`.
4. `routes/cards.ts`: `GET /s/:shareId` (OG and Farcaster mini app embed meta, the card preview, the
   "Play today's Flocked" link carrying the sharer's `ref` and the share ID, "See the round" after
   the reveal and never before), `GET /cards/:shareId/:variant.png` and
   `GET /rounds/:id/card/:variant.png` (404 until the mode reveals; `requireRoundAccess`), each
   serving from R2 with cache headers and rendering on demand when the image is missing; the edge
   cache purge on a re-render; `share_landing` into `ANALYTICS` with the `ref`.
5. Migration `0023`: `share_cards.rendered_at` (the handoff's recommendation), with its
   `test/migrations.test.ts` row.
6. PIPE-1 (cards half) and AC-7 tests.
