## Objective
Build `wave-9.md` "W9-B": the verification library and the `flocked-verify` CLI. Fetch the manifest and chunks, check chunk hashes and totals, verify the drand signature for `beaconRound`, re-decrypt Free ciphertexts (Stakes ciphertexts from `Entered` logs), recompute every VOID reason, re-run `@flocked/settle` and both Merkle roots, and compare against the onchain proposal (Stakes) or the anchored commitment and manifest (Free).
