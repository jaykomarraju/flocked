On the merged local stack, prove the remaining Stakes browser path: a player verifies personhood in the browser through the Coinbase mock and enters Stakes (E2E-1 Stakes UI), claims after the challenge window (E2E-4 UI), and sees a vetoed settlement turn into refunds (E2E-5).
