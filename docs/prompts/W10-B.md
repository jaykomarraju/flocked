## Objective
Build `wave-10.md` "W10-B": `apps/watcher`, a Worker in its own wrangler project for a separate Cloudflare account, with its own D1, RPC and drand access, no shared bindings, and no shared code except the packages `@flocked/verify`, `@flocked/settle`, `@flocked/tlock` and `@flocked/abi`. For Stakes it checks `RoundCreated` against the config published at lock (snapshotted independently at lock time) and the backend-independent rules (a house question's creator is the treasury, else the creator's attestations; defaults unless an override was published before lock); proposals by full recompute through `@flocked/verify` (payout leaves against derived amounts, VOID recompute); voids and `refundTooFew`. It signs verdicts and pages on a mismatch, on unable, or on a missing verdict 30 minutes after a proposal.
