## Summary
- **`e2e/ui/stakes-round.spec.ts`** (E2E-1 Stakes UI, E2E-4 UI, AC-5's UI half; four `ui` tests and one `@warp`
  test sharing one round and three browser contexts): each player signs in through the dialog with a Coinbase Smart
  Wallet stand-in, verifies personhood in the tab (Today's Stakes toggle, "Verify with Coinbase", the sheet's age and
  terms, the Coinbase mock's OAuth flow, the API's callback), sees the round's fixed stake and its special rules, and
  enters as one sponsored user operation: pending ("Confirming") until anvil includes `approve` then `enter`. The
  round closes, reveals and is proposed on its own; each browser shows unsealing, "Counting the flock…" and its
  provisional result ("Provisional. Final at h:mm", "Payout, once final" / "Lost"), which matches `GET /rounds/:id/me`;
  Claims lists each payout as "Opens h:mm" with "Claim all" disabled. In `ui-warp` the chain, the game clock and the
  reopened browsers move to the end of the window: the coordinator finalizes, and each player claims from Claims in
  one sponsored `claim` batch, exactly what `/claims` lists (the winner its payout, the losers only their rebate).
- **`e2e/ui/stakes-veto.spec.ts`** (E2E-5, AC-5 "a guardian veto of a settlement results in a full refund"; five `ui`
  tests, no warp): three players verify and enter in the browser, the backend proposes a settlement each browser
  shows as provisional, the guardian vetoes it on the escrow (anvil sends as the stack's guardian, as E2E-6 does), and
  each page — never reloaded — redraws its result as "Refunded" with the veto line (reason 5) and "Returned 1 USDC";
  the recorded phases are unsealing → counting → revealed → refunded. Each player then claims the stake back from
  Claims in one sponsored `claimRefund`.
- **`e2e/fixtures/`**: `browser.ts` gains `installSmartWallet` (EIP-5792 through the app's real paymaster proxy and
  the provider mock, then the calls on anvil, pending until mined), `verifyInBrowser`, `recordReveal`/`revealSeen`,
  `clockTime`, `newPlayerPage(…, storageState)` and a block on the Coinbase SDK's telemetry; `clock.ts`
  `produceBlocks`; `rounds.ts` `waitForToday`; `wallet.ts` `giveUsdc`.
- **Five fixes** (`41c2baa`, `ddd95f1`; Deviations): with W10-C's mocked world no browser Stakes entry, claim or veto
  refund could have worked on the real stack. Each has a test that fails without it.
- **`docs/prompts/W10-Z.md`** from plan.md 3.1 and 3.4 and the `wave-10.md` "W10-Z checklist".

## Deviations
- **Five app fixes outside the prompt's file list** (`41c2baa`, `ddd95f1`; an integrate session fixes integration
  breaks, and the rows could not pass without them; none is a registry file). Each was invisible to W10-C's mocked
  world (`e2e/ui/world.ts` accepts any `/prepare` body, never opens a ciphertext and has no indexer lag):
  1. `features/stakes/eligibility.ts`: a new account's `/me` reasons are `not_verified`, `age_unattested` and
     `tos_not_accepted` together (probed on the stack), and the client offered "Verify with Coinbase" only for
     `not_verified` alone, so Stakes was hidden from every new player. The two the verification sheet stores now keep
     the verify path beside `not_verified`. W10-C's "hides Stakes when another reason sits beside not_verified" no longer
     loops over those two; it gained the case where a hiding reason sits beside them, and a new test covers the
     triplet (fails without the fix).
  2. `features/stakes/entry.ts`: `/prepare` got wagmi's checksummed wallet; `AddressSchema` is lowercase only, so
     every entry was a 400. Lowercased, with a test (fails without it).
  3. `crypto/entry.ts` `sealPick`: the plaintext's round reference was always the round's ULID; a Stakes plaintext
     names the onchain round ID (spec "Sealed picks (timelock encryption)"), so every browser Stakes entry settled VOID
     (`bad_plaintext`, 0/0 tally, rule-1 refund). `sealPick` takes the mode; a test opens what `submitStakesEntry`
     sealed with the recorded quicknet signature (fails without it). W10-C's "carries the ciphertext onchain byte for
     byte" only asserted `expect(() => decryptWithSignature).not.toThrow()`, which can't fail.
  4. `features/claims/Claims.tsx`: after a batch landed, Claims refetched once, but `/claims` drops a payout only when
     the indexer has seen its `Claimed` (behind the safe head; one stack claim took over 120 s), so the claimed row came
     back as "Ready" until a reload, inviting a second claim that reverts. Claims now hides payouts whose claim landed
     and asks again every 3 s while `/claims` lists them; a test serves the stale list once (fails without it).
  5. `apps/api/src/read/claims.ts`: after a guardian veto of a settlement the escrow is Refunded at once, but `/claims`
     gave the refund the vetoed proposal's `claimsOpenAt` (two hours on), so Claims said "Opens 10:19pm" for a
     refund the escrow already pays. A refund is claimable from `final_at` unless it is still a refund proposal;
     `test/read/claims.test.ts` › "lists the refund as claimable from the veto, not from the vetoed window's end"
     (fails without it).
- **No axe on the pending entry**: injecting axe while the user operation is out outlasted the app's 90 s inclusion
  wait (every status poll pending, the transactions mined after the timeout), as W10-C found in `stakes-entry.spec.ts`.
  The pending screen is checked by text; its accessibility is the `core/stakes-entry/pending` artboard's.
- **The personhood callback lands on Settings**, a placeholder until W12; `verifyInBrowser` asserts that landing
  (`/settings?personhood=verified`) and takes the nav back to Today (spec issue 2). Not changed: Settings is a
  verification surface in the spec too.
- **World setup, not seam calls**: each player's Coinbase mock account and its wallet's EAS attestation (the world it
  verifies against; a fresh account each, chosen on the mock's authorize page), 1 USDC minted to its wallet by an
  anvil dev account (what it arrives with), blocks mined every 2 s while a step waits on the indexer (anvil mines
  none of its own), and the guardian's veto sent as the guardian. Every player action is the browser's.
- **The smart-wallet stand-in** sends the batch's calls in order from the wallet's address (anvil has no EntryPoint or
  smart-wallet contract), only after the proxy sponsored both `pm_` calls; it reads receipts directly, because viem's
  `waitForTransactionReceipt` shares one block watcher across concurrent waiters and anvil mines no later block.
- **CI's `ui` job is two steps** (`c34a8b2`): `stakes-round.spec.ts` brings the first `@warp` test into the UI suite;
  with `ui-warp` selecting it, Playwright ran `ui` as its dependency, which ignores CLI filters, so `--grep-invert
  @screenshot` stopped applying and the Mac-only screenshot tests ran on Linux (E2E 40431489871: 181 tests, 27
  screenshot failures, every other test green). Now `--project=ui`, then `--project=ui-warp --no-deps`, each with the
  grep (62 tests), as the `api` job does. Locally, `--project=ui --project=ui-warp <file>` runs the whole `ui` suite for
  the same reason; run a spec's phases as `--project=ui <file>`, then `--project=ui-warp --no-deps <file>`.
- No test was skipped, deleted or weakened, apart from the one axe call above and the eligibility loop in fix 1.

## Spec issues
1. **AC-8 vs "Client app" › Today:** AC-8 says the Stakes UI is absent for users who are not verified; Today shows them
   Stakes with "Verify with Coinbase" (W10-C built the latter). Recommend AC-8 read "not verified (shown with Verify
   with Coinbase instead)". Also decide what a *verified* player whose terms version changed sees: `/me` says
   `tos_not_accepted` alone and Stakes is hidden with no way back in; recommend the sheet open for `tos_not_accepted`
   or `age_unattested` alone.
2. **"Identity and personhood" › Personhood / "Client app" › Entry flow (Stakes):** the callback always redirects to
   `/settings?personhood=…` (`personhood/config.ts`), but verification starts from Today's sheet, and Settings is a
   placeholder until W12; nothing shows the outcome (`failed&reason=` included). Recommend `POST /me/personhood` carry
   where the flow began (Today or Settings) through the OAuth state and return there with the outcome shown; until W12
   at least return to Today.
3. **"Client app" › Claims:** Claims decides "Ready" by the device's clock (`useServerNow()` with no skew), so a device
   behind the chain shows "Opens h:mm" after the window. Recommend the socket's skew, or a server `claimable` flag.

## Open issues
1. **Coinbase Wallet SDK telemetry:** the app's wagmi config posts to `cca-lite.coinbase.com` (`/amp`, `/metrics`) from
   every page; the test browser saw it as 403s, and `preparePage` now blocks it. A privacy and consent question for the
   web (disable the SDK's telemetry, or say so in the privacy notice). Owner: W11-D or W14-B.
2. **The DST notice fires for any round not 24 h long** (`features/today/copy.ts` `dstNotice`): every stack round reads
   "Clocks go forward tonight. So this round runs 0 hours." Harmless for daily rounds; wrong for room rounds or any
   custom length. Owner: W11-C (rooms in the web).
3. **The mocked world can't catch these breaks again:** `world.ts`'s `/prepare` route could parse
   `StakesPrepareRequestSchema`, and W10-C's "carries the ciphertext onchain byte for byte" could open its ciphertext
   (fix 3's test does). Small, for Z or W11.
4. **The smart-wallet stand-in is not atomic:** it sends a batch's calls one by one (anvil has no EntryPoint), so an
   `enter` that reverts after its `approve` leaves the approval; the browser specs exercise the success path, the
   proxy's refusal stays E2E-11's. The Today card's final (non-provisional) state after the window isn't checked on the
   stack (W10-C's `reveal.test.tsx` covers it); Claims' "Opens" → "Ready" is.
5. **CI time:** the `ui` job went from 7.8 to 22.4 min (locally `pnpm e2e:ui` from 14.1 to 21.7 min; the two specs are
   about 13 min), inside each step's 40-min `globalTimeout` and the 45-min job; split the `ui` step if it passes 35 min.
6. `e2e/ui/README.md`'s spec table has no rows for W10-C's three specs or these two (not this session's file; small).
