## Summary
- **Chain, indexer, settlement:**
  - the operator nonce allocator fills a nonce a reorg freed (R1);
  - a re-mined `OutcomeProposed` restores its record (R2);
  - removals of `ProposalVetoed`, `RoundFinalized` and `RoundRefunded` put D1 back: status, `final_at`, the record and unclaimed payouts (R3);
  - `applyStakes` writes once per proposal (R4);
  - a primary RPC with no archive state falls back like a mismatch (R5);
  - an `Entered` removal deletes its `refund` payout (R11).
- **O1:** a refund-proposal veto alerts and opens (or reopens) a `refund_vetoed` hold. No new proposal posts while any hold is open: the coordinator checks, and `applyStakes` checks again so a veto indexed between them can't race it. `refundTimeout` is the backstop. Migration `0016` and the seam route `POST /__test/holds/release` support it.
- **Reads:**
  - Free credits are computed before `revealed_at` (R6);
  - the edge cache keys on the path plus the validated, sorted query (R7);
  - Stakes proofs are served only for the live proposal: the record's bundle equals the chain's, the rebuilt root equals the record's, and the status is `settle_proposed`/`settled` (R8, R9);
  - non-members get 404 on `POST /entries` and `/prepare` (R10);
  - `?wallet=` adds `roomClaims` (O5).
- **Tickets, lock:**
  - the signer refuses an expiry more than 300 + 60 s ahead (R12);
  - a used ticket blocks any new ticket for its round (R13);
  - the cap counts an unused ticket until the indexer passes its expiry (O2);
  - an unknown region needs 21 if `age21` names a region of its country (O3);
  - `payoutAddress` is frozen into `config_json.stakes` at lock (O4);
  - a signer kill-switch refusal answers `stakes_disabled`, and a failed DELETE is retried (optional items).

## Spec issues
- **"Round lifecycle" (Stakes rows):** nothing says what a reorged-out `OutcomeProposed`, `ProposalVetoed`, `RoundFinalized` or `RoundRefunded` does. Recommend: its D1 effect is undone (status, `final_at`, the record, unclaimed payouts) and it alerts. Removing a refund veto leaves its `refund_vetoed` hold open for the guardian.
- **"Data model" › `settlement_holds`:** say that a second `refund_vetoed` veto reopens the released row with a new `created_at` (it is in the Decision log only).
- **"Identity and personhood" › Account deletion and the API `/claims` row:** the text says "chain round ID, amount and proof only". `roomClaims` also carries `kind` (needed for `claim(roundId, kind, proof)`), `account` (the queried wallet) and `claimsOpenAt` (public onchain). Recommend naming these fields and the array.
- **"Identity and personhood" › Entry tickets (O2):**
  - The indexer stores no block timestamp for its position, so "processed past its expiry" is a lower bound: the newest mirrored escrow event at or below `indexer_state.last_block_number`, plus 1 s per block after it, held to 1 s before the oldest undispatched event.
  - In a quiet period an expired ticket counts up to about twice the time since the last escrow event. With no mirrored event at all, it counts for the rest of its game day.
  - Recommend storing the position's block timestamp in `indexer_state` (a migration plus `indexer/core.ts`).
- **"Settlement and payout math" › creator at lock:** the author-wallet checks (verified, linked, attestations) are not implemented at lock. `creator` comes from the admin draft, and `payoutAddress` is the author's Settings wallet only when it equals that creator. Whoever resolves the creator should set both at lock.

## Open issues
- **`OutcomeProposed.payoutRoot` is never compared in D1.** The handler checks only `bundleHash`. The bundle hash binds the root through the manifest, and reads check the rebuilt root against the record's, but an explicit check plus alert in `indexer/handlers/stakes.ts` would be cheap (W7-D or a later fix).
- **A reorg that drops a mined operator transaction:** its purpose (`propose`, `finalize`, …) keeps answering from its `mined` row and isn't re-sent. R1 only frees the nonce. A W13-B candidate (alerted today by the removal's alert).
- **`src/indexer/holds.ts` types:** `HoldReason` is typed `'foreign_entry'` only and `txHash` non-null, while `void_rate`/`refund_vetoed` rows have a NULL `tx_hash`. Types only, no behaviour bug.
- **R9 scope:** after a reorged proposal `/me` still shows the entry's option and the result, because `revealed_at` stays set and the tally was public. Only payouts and proofs are gated, as asked.
- **The RoundDO's own `not_member` answer** (`round-do/entries.test.ts`) could become `not_found` (W7-A's file).
