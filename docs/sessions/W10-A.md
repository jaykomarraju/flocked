## Deviations

- **satori 0.32.0, not the current 0.62.0.** From 0.33 satori imports `harfbuzzjs`, whose emscripten
  glue calls `WebAssembly.instantiate` on bytes at import time; workerd refuses that ("Wasm code
  generation disallowed by embedder"), so the module cannot load in a Worker at all — with
  `satori/standalone` either. 0.32.0 is the last release without it, and it takes its layout engine
  as a module (`satori/standalone`'s `init(yoga.wasm)`), which workerd does allow.
- **The fonts are imported as base64, not as files.** `wrangler.jsonc` (W10-D's) would need a `Data`
  module rule for a `.ttf` import, and Vite — which builds the Workers tests — resolves one to a URL
  string instead. `assets/fonts/build.mjs` writes `src/cards/fonts.gen.ts` (353 KB of base64, about
  265 KB of TTF); both bundlers treat that identically. The TTFs stay in the repo as the source.
- **Inter is instanced at `opsz` 24**, not its default 14: Paper renders the artboards with
  `opsz: auto`, and at 14 the refund card's why line came out wide enough to wrap a line the export
  keeps on one. See `assets/fonts/README.md`.
- **The pixel comparison runs against committed fixtures**, each export box-filtered to a fifth of
  the card by `test/cards/fixtures/build.mjs`. The exports are ~300 KB each and a Workers test has no
  filesystem, so every reference byte has to be bundled into the test. The fixture set is 10 of the
  54 card exports — enough for every distinct layout, not every state.
- **Mean pixel error is a bound, not a fingerprint.** At a fifth scale a card is mostly background,
  and the residual error (the two text engines' advance widths drifting a few pixels along a line) is
  about as large as the error between two different states' cards: a win rendered against the
  headcount-tie fixture scores 13.6 where the tie's own card scores 12.6. So `pixel.test.ts` bounds
  the difference (mae < 16, over < 14%) and `layout.test.ts` is what tells the states apart — band
  positions within 6 px and the accent's share within half a point, with five negative controls.
- **The `embed` and `square` refund title sizes are inferred.** Paper gave the `og` refund artboards
  a 72 px title (confirmed); `embed` 76 and `square` 92 keep the same ratio to their result lines and
  are not covered by a fixture. The `embed` geometry as a whole was derived from the exports (its
  bands match within 1 px) rather than read off the artboard.
- **The teaser's big line breaks between its two sentences**, as the artboard draws it ("I sealed my
  pick." / "Can you out-think the flock?"), rather than wrapping on width.
- **The question and result stepping is decided from estimated advance widths**, not from a trial
  render: a daily round renders three cards per settled player inside five minutes, so a render per
  step is too expensive. The tables are calibrated to four widths measured off the exports and held
  there by `fit.test.ts`, and they deliberately run 2–4% wide, because satori's text is a shade wider
  than the browser's that drew the artboards and an under-estimate clips a card. Two consequences:
  the Stakes amount card lands at 72 px where the artboard sets 76 (at 76 the line does not in fact
  fit beside the sheep here), and the round card's line at 80 px where the artboard sets 88. Both are
  inside the layout test's tolerance; a narrower Inter or a measured-advance implementation would
  close the gap.
- **Stepping is four pixels at a time and never past the floor**, so `og`'s 42 px question bottoms
  out at 34 rather than exactly 32 (the result line reaches 56 exactly from all three sizes).

- **Over size M, and split at the prompt's own seam.** Matching the artboards took far more context
  than the budget allowed: reading the three variant artboards out of Paper, deriving the `embed`
  geometry from the exports, and then the empirical loop of render, measure, correct. The session
  stops at a green commit with the renderer and its tests done (plan.md 2.6, 2.8) and continues as
  `W10-A.2` with the split the prompt names: the consumer, the share page, the routes, the
  re-renders and the analytics.

## Spec issues

- **Who creates a teaser `share_cards` row.** A teaser has to exist before the reveal (the Sealed
  screen's share sheet links `/s/:shareId`), but no enqueue point in this session's scope writes one:
  the entry path is `routes/entries.ts`. Recommendation: enqueue a `cards` message with
  `kind: 'teaser'` when an entry is sealed, which is one line in the entries route — D's or a later
  wave's, named under "Notes for D". Spec heading: "Share cards and distribution" › Share surfaces.
- **Room-round share pages.** Cards render for room rounds (the `too_few_entrants` refund artboard
  uses a 2-player room round), but `/s/:shareId` and `/cards/:shareId/*.png` are public by design, so
  a room round's share page would publish member-only per-option data ("API" › Reveal gating: "Room
  rounds are for members only on every round route, WebSocket and bundle"). Recommendation: no
  `share_cards` row for a room round, and keep its card at `/rounds/:id/card/*` behind
  `requireRoundAccess`. Spec headings: "Share cards and distribution" › Share surfaces, "API" ›
  Reveal gating.
- **`REFUND_LINES` is duplicated.** The refund lines live in `apps/web/src/features/reveal/copy.ts`,
  which a Worker cannot import, so `src/cards/model.ts` has `CARD_REFUND_LINES` with the same eight
  lines. They belong in `@flocked/shared` (`copy.ts`, beside `VOICE`), which neither W10-A nor W10-C
  owns this wave. Same for `refundKeepsSplit`.
