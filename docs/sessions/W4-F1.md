## Summary
- **Void:** at close a void is abandoned only on proof (head read first, then a definite final push; a thrown error or an unconfirmed taken nonce is never proof); the blind 20 s grace is local only. Our own void landing after abandonment alerts (and Stakes is voided as for a guardian void). A void that can't confirm alerts once before close (reverted, cancelled, or refused 5 times). RoundDO RangeErrors in handlers alert and return.
- **TxSender:** `onTxSent` before every (re)broadcast; bumps only from the version a node holds, never past `TX_MAX_FEE_GWEI` (default 5 gwei), versions capped at 32; receipts read only once the nonce moved; a taken nonce needs two sightings a block apart.
- **Operator:** an abandoned `createRound` (pending at opensAt) or `voidRound` (abandoned at close) is marked, then replaced by a 0-value self-send at its nonce; the scheduler tick drives every pending cancellation (`operator_txs.status = 'cancelled'`).
- **AnchorDO:** per-version kind and keys; an early manifest revert is retried; a resubmitted confirmed key is read onchain again and resent if gone (the indexer's handler resubmits a reorged-out `Locked` / `Committed`); every confirmation path records its tx hash and `round_modes` value; a sent lock stays in its replacement until closesAt; a write that lands after its key failed still confirms.
- **Indexer (subagent):** removals queue (`chain_event_removals`) dispatched before additions, per handler; a slot change is removal + revision + 2; checkpoints for deep reorgs; range regrowth and per-chunk apply; constructor exemption tied to code absence; repeating poison-row alert with ALERT-8 independent; head-age lag; every log block checked; per-poll budgets.

- **Deep reorgs:** a reorg deeper than the retained checkpoints (about 64k blocks) rescans from the deploy block within the per-poll budget.
